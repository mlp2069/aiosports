How streams are picked

Ranking, kind and how far behind live
Rank them by
Rating ranks every stream on what was measured about it — the resolution and bitrate read from the stream itself, and whether that source has been answering lately. Source order hands you your own order from Streaming sources instead, best stream first within each source. Dragging your sources into an order selects it on its own.
Show first
Direct streams play in the app. Web streams open a browser. No preference leaves them ranked on quality alone.
Extra buffer
A stream that never quite breaks but keeps catching itself is usually a source publishing in bursts. Starting further behind the live edge rides those out, at the cost of seeing everything that much later. Off plays as close to live as the source allows.
Where you watch
A few sources disguise their video as images. The TV and Android apps play it anyway; the desktop and iPhone apps refuse it, so for them the server hands the video over cleanly. Automatic recognises which app is asking, so one install works on all of them.
